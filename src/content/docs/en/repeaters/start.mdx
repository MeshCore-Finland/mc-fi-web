## The essentials before you start

The most important thing to understand is that **LoRa, the radio technology used by MeshCore, is slow**. Unlike the Internet, LoRa radios have very limited bandwidth. We hope everyone joining the community understands this constraint so we can avoid the excesses seen in mesh network projects around the world.

Radio communications in Finland and elsewhere in Europe must comply with European ([CEPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Conference_of_Postal_and_Telecommunications_Administrations)) and Finnish ([Traficom](https://traficom.fi/fi/radioluvat-ja-taajuudet)) regulations. These rules **must also be followed** when using MeshCore.

The essentials are to stay within permitted frequency bands and radiated power limits, and to follow the EU-wide rule that **a single device may transmit for no more than 10% of any rolling one-hour period**. Using the recommended settings meets all these requirements.

It is also useful to know that signals in the licence-free 868 MHz band do not readily pass through substantial obstacles such as terrain or concrete walls.

## What this guide covers

- The first two pages cover choosing repeater hardware and installing firmware. Some details differ from companion radios.
- The third page helps you configure a device once its firmware is installed. You can also quickly check settings in the <DocLink page="faq/general">FAQ</DocLink>.
- The fourth page covers the important topic of Region Scopes. Finland's community has strong recommendations, so it is worth reading: skipping scopes may mean your repeater's traffic is not forwarded by others.
