Pirkanmaa has Finland's first organised MeshCore community and a well-developed repeater network.

- [Mesh Pirkanmaa website](https://meshpirkanmaa.org/finnish.html)
- Open Matrix room: `#mesh-pirkanmaa:hacklab.fi`
- The IRCnet channel `#mesh-pirkanmaa` is bridged to Matrix, so you can also join over IRC.
