:::note
A distinctive feature of Southern Finland is our **continuous two-way mesh connection to Estonia**, and through it toward the other Baltic countries and Central Europe.

**This is why we strongly encourage using the `fi` region scope on all repeaters and channels**, so local traffic does not unnecessarily consume other countries' repeater capacity and airtime.
:::

## Where to find us

Southern Finland's enthusiasts do not yet have a separate chat space. The easiest way to find us is through the <DocLink page="communities/start">main community channels</DocLink>.

## Helsinki metropolitan area

The Helsinki metropolitan area (Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa and Kauniainen) has an active community, but the network is still somewhat fragmented. New repeaters appear weekly, if not daily. Connections to Estonia are good, with several repeaters on both sides of the gulf taking part. This puts the metropolitan area at the forefront of adopting the `fi` region scope on repeaters and channels.

## Vihti and Lohja

There are many enthusiasts and well-placed repeaters here. There is no direct connection to Espoo yet, but traffic often travels via the Estonian coast, especially between Vihti and the metropolitan area.

## Hyvinkää, Riihimäki and Loppi

A very active community is building the network here. It is not yet connected to Vantaa or to Pirkanmaa via Hämeenlinna, but that is only a matter of time.

## Kotka

Kotka's first repeater is on the map: a start! There are no connections to the rest of the mesh yet.

## Connections elsewhere

There is currently no connection to Pirkanmaa or Southwest Finland.
