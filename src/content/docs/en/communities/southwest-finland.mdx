## Turku

Turku has an active and rapidly growing community whose repeater network already covers almost the entire city, from the centre and the Raisio border towards Varissuo and Kaarina. Coverage is actively measured with MeshMapper, and the network is currently expanding into outlying areas such as Hirvensalo and neighbouring municipalities.

See also the [Vasumesh website](https://vasumesh.fi/).
